DebtDown

Multiple debts, one extra payment a month - where it goes changes everything. The wrong payoff order quietly costs thousands in interest. DebtDown simulates both strategies and shows the difference in dollars and months.

Run the numbers

List each debt with its balance, APR and minimum payment, plus the extra you can pay monthly. DebtDown simulates the avalanche (highest APR first) against the snowball (smallest balance first): months to debt-free, total interest, exact payoff order, and a plain verdict on which wins and by how much. Everything stays in your browser.
